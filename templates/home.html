Good to know

Questions, answered clearly.

A few things to know before you share, request, or deliver food.

Who can join AhaarBonton?

Donors, NGOs, and volunteers can create an account. Choose the role that matches how you want to participate; each workspace is tailored to that responsibility.

How is food safety handled?

Donors provide the food condition, packaging details, and a safe expiry deadline. Urgent posts are prioritized, while pickup and delivery are confirmed through one-time codes.

Is my exact location public?

Map pins are rounded for privacy, but the pickup address or landmark you enter appears on food listings. Share only the detail needed for coordination, and request participants can use the workflow to arrange the handover.

Does it cost anything to participate?

Joining and using the core food-sharing workflow is free. AhaarBonton is designed to reduce food waste and strengthen community access, not to create a barrier to participation.
